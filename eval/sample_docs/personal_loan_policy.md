# Northwind Example Bank — Personal Loan Policy (fictional sample document)

This document is invented for testing the retrieval pipeline. It does not describe any real bank.

## 1. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years of age at the time of application. Salaried applicants need a minimum of two years of total work experience, with at least six months at their current employer. Self-employed applicants must show three years of continuous business operation, supported by filed income tax returns.

The minimum net monthly income is 25,000 rupees for applicants in metro cities and 18,000 rupees for applicants in all other locations. Applicants with a credit score below 650 are not eligible for an unsecured personal loan. Applicants with a score between 650 and 700 may be approved only with a co-applicant.

## 2. Loan amount and tenure

The minimum loan amount is 50,000 rupees and the maximum is 25,00,000 rupees. The loan tenure ranges from 12 months to 60 months. The total of all monthly instalments, including the new loan, must not exceed 55 percent of the applicant's net monthly income. This limit is called the fixed obligation to income ratio, or FOIR.

## 3. Interest rates

Interest rates are fixed for the full tenure. The rate depends on the credit score band:

- Credit score 800 and above: 10.5 percent per year
- Credit score 750 to 799: 11.75 percent per year
- Credit score 700 to 749: 13.5 percent per year
- Credit score 650 to 699 (with co-applicant): 15.25 percent per year

## 4. Fees and charges

A processing fee of 1.5 percent of the loan amount is charged, subject to a minimum of 2,000 rupees, plus applicable taxes. A late payment charge of 2 percent per month is applied to the overdue instalment amount. A cheque or mandate bounce attracts a flat charge of 500 rupees per instance.

## 5. Prepayment and foreclosure

Part-prepayment is allowed after 6 instalments have been paid, up to a maximum of 25 percent of the outstanding principal in a financial year. Full foreclosure is allowed after 12 instalments. The foreclosure charge is 4 percent of the outstanding principal if the loan is closed between month 13 and month 24, and 2 percent after month 24. There is no foreclosure charge for loans closed using the borrower's own funds after month 36.

## 6. Documents required

Applicants must submit proof of identity, proof of address, the last three months of salary slips, and the last six months of bank statements. Self-employed applicants submit the last two years of income tax returns and audited financial statements instead of salary slips.

## 7. Disbursal

Approved loans are disbursed to the applicant's verified savings account within 2 working days of signing the loan agreement. Disbursal to a third-party account is not permitted.
