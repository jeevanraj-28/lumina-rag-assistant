# Northwind Example Bank — Payments and KYC Guidelines (fictional sample document)

This document is invented for testing the retrieval pipeline. It does not describe any real bank or regulation.

## Know Your Customer (KYC)

Every customer must complete KYC before an account can be used for payments. Low-risk customers must update their KYC every 10 years, medium-risk customers every 8 years, and high-risk customers every 2 years. A customer whose KYC has expired can receive money but cannot make outward payments until the update is complete.

Video-based KYC is available for individual customers. The video call must be recorded, must include a live photograph of the customer, and must be conducted by a trained official of the bank. It is not available for business accounts.

## Payment limits

The default daily limit for transfers through the mobile app is 2,00,000 rupees. Customers can raise it up to 10,00,000 rupees after a cooling period of 24 hours. A newly added beneficiary can receive at most 50,000 rupees in the first 24 hours after being added.

## Failed and reversed transactions

If an account is debited but the payment fails, the amount is automatically reversed within 1 working day. If the reversal is delayed beyond that, the customer is compensated at 100 rupees per day of delay.

## Suspicious transactions

Transactions that do not match a customer's known profile are flagged for review. Examples include sudden large cash deposits, many small transfers to unrelated accounts in a short time, and payments to high-risk jurisdictions. Flagged transactions are reviewed by the compliance team within 7 days. Staff must never tell the customer that a transaction has been flagged, because this could alert someone attempting money laundering.

## Dormant accounts

An account with no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months is classified as dormant. A dormant account can be reactivated by completing a fresh KYC update at any branch or through video KYC. No charge is levied for reactivation.

## Customer complaints

Payment-related complaints must be acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 30 days. If the customer is not satisfied, the complaint is escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer.
