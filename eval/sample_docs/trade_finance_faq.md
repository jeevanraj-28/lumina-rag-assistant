# Northwind Example Bank — Trade Finance FAQ (fictional sample document)

This document is invented for testing the retrieval pipeline. It does not describe any real bank.

## What is a letter of credit?

A letter of credit is a written promise by the buyer's bank to pay the seller, provided the seller presents documents that match the terms of the credit exactly. It protects the seller against the risk that the buyer will not pay, and it protects the buyer because payment is made only against the agreed shipping documents.

## How long does it take to issue a letter of credit?

Once a complete application and the required margin are received, an import letter of credit is issued within 1 working day. Amendments to an existing credit are processed within the same working day if received before 2 pm.

## What margin is required?

Customers with a sanctioned trade limit are not required to deposit margin. Customers without a sanctioned limit must deposit a cash margin of 100 percent of the credit value before issuance.

## What documents are usually presented under an export letter of credit?

The commonly required documents are the commercial invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading or airway bill, the certificate of origin and the insurance certificate. Any discrepancy, such as a spelling difference in the consignee name, can lead to the documents being refused.

## What happens if the documents have a discrepancy?

The bank informs the customer within 5 working days of presentation. The customer can correct and re-present the documents before the credit expires, or ask the applicant to accept the discrepancy. A discrepancy fee of 75 US dollars is charged per set of documents.

## What is invoice discounting?

Invoice discounting lets a seller receive up to 80 percent of the value of an accepted invoice immediately, instead of waiting for the buyer to pay at the end of the credit period. The remaining amount, less interest and fees, is paid to the seller when the buyer settles the invoice.

## What is the maximum tenure for invoice discounting?

The maximum tenure is 180 days from the invoice date. Invoices older than 30 days on the date of the request are not eligible.

## What is a bank guarantee?

A bank guarantee is an undertaking by the bank to pay a beneficiary if the customer fails to meet a contractual obligation. Performance guarantees and advance payment guarantees are the most common types. The commission is charged quarterly at 0.5 percent of the guarantee amount.
